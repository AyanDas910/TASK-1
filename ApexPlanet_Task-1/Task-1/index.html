A SIMPLE START

Three little habits.
Lots of life.

01

Find the right light

Notice how sunlight moves through your room. Most houseplants enjoy bright, indirect light.

02

Check before you water

Touch the soil first. A quick check helps you avoid watering on autopilot.

03

Give it a little attention

Look for new leaves, dust gently, and rotate your pot now and then for even growth.
